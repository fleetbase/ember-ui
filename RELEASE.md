> v0.4.7 ~ "Column picker groups"

---
## Highlights

- **Column picker groups.** `VisibleColumnPicker` (the Customize Columns dropdown of every table) lists columns that carry a `group` under a heading of that name, after the ungrouped columns and in the order the groups first appear. Extensions that add columns to another engine's table can keep theirs together instead of mixing them into the table's own list; fleetbase/ember-core v0.3.27 adds `TableColumn.inGroup()` to set it. Columns without a group are listed exactly as before.
